Ground breaks on Fuelstor's new terminal, a bet on Djibouti's energy future

Leadership from Fuelstor and Salaam Group joined local officials this month to break ground on the Fuelstor Oil & Gas Terminal Project. The site, laid out with rows of storage tanks, a dedicated jetty access and a modern loading yard, is designed to expand the country's fuel storage capacity and strengthen supply reliability for industry and logistics operators across the region. The ceremony closed with a ribbon-cutting attended by the project's partners and Group leadership.

Finance

Salaam Bank expands its role in local economic development with 20 commercial premises in Ali-Sabieh

In October 2025, Salaam Bank and the Ali-Sabieh Regional Council launched construction of 20 new commercial premises, built to give local entrepreneurs, artisans and small businesses a place to trade. By July 2026 the project was complete, and Salaam Bank held a consultation with the future occupants to walk through how the premises would operate and hear their expectations. It's a public-private partnership that goes beyond a banking product — physical infrastructure paired with local economic development, with the bank helping build an ecosystem around small businesses rather than simply financing them.
